# Thermodynamics & Statistical Mechanics

**VDM Context:** H-theorem, entropy production, metriplectic M-structure, thermodynamic routing.

## Essential Reading

1. **"Statistical Mechanics"** by R. K. Pathria and Paul D. Beale (3rd Edition, 2011)  
   - Academic Press
   - ISBN: 978-0123821881
   - Comprehensive graduate-level text
   - Equilibrium and nonequilibrium statistical mechanics

2. **"The Principles of Statistical Mechanics"** by Richard C. Tolman (1938, Dover reprint 1979)  
   - Dover Publications
   - ISBN: 978-0486638966
   - Classic foundational work
   - H-theorem, ensemble theory

3. **"Thermodynamics and an Introduction to Thermostatistics"** by Herbert B. Callen (2nd Edition, 1985)  
   - Wiley
   - ISBN: 978-0471862567
   - Axiomatic approach, fundamental principles

4. **"Non-Equilibrium Thermodynamics"** by S. R. de Groot and P. Mazur (1962, Dover reprint 1984)  
   - Dover Publications
   - ISBN: 978-0486647418
   - Linear irreversible thermodynamics
   - Onsager relations, entropy production

5. **"Introduction to Modern Statistical Mechanics"** by David Chandler (1987)  
   - Oxford University Press
   - ISBN: 978-0195042771
   - Concise, physical approach to stat mech

### Nonequilibrium & Entropy Production

6. **"Beyond Equilibrium Thermodynamics"** by Hans Christian Öttinger (2005)  
   - Wiley-Interscience
   - ISBN: 978-0471666585
   - GENERIC framework, time evolution, dissipation

7. **"Thermodynamics of Irreversible Processes"** by Ilya Prigogine (1967)  
   - Wiley-Interscience
   - ISBN: 978-0470699904
   - Minimum entropy production, stability theory

8. **"Self-Organization in Nonequilibrium Systems"** by Grégoire Nicolis and Ilya Prigogine (1977)  
   - Wiley-Interscience
   - ISBN: 978-0471024019
   - Dissipative structures, pattern formation
