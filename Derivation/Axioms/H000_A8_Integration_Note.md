# H000 ↔ A8 Integration Note
## Primitive Bifurcation Hypothesis as a Deeper Antecedent to the Lietz Hierarchy Candidate

**Document type:** Migration / integration note  
**Owner:** Justin K. Lietz  
**Status:** Proposal-grade  
**Purpose:** Decide whether the Primitive Bifurcation Hypothesis should overwrite A8, sit beneath A8, or be merged into a revised unified hierarchy-origin candidate.

---

## Executive recommendation

**Do not overwrite A8 yet.**  
Introduce the Primitive Bifurcation Hypothesis (H000) as a deeper antecedent hypothesis beneath the current A8 candidate, preserve A8’s operational predictions for now, and pursue a later bridge theorem showing whether A8 is one domain-level realization of the earlier bifurcation-driven hierarchy engine.

That is the least contaminating and most reversible move.

---

## 1. Why not overwrite A8 immediately

The current A8 candidate already lives in a much later physical regime. It assumes:

- scalar field structure,
- tachyonic onset \(V''(0) < 0\),
- metriplectic evolution,
- pulled-front solutions,
- exponential tails,
- finite-excess-energy scaling,
- codimension-1 interfaces,
- boundary energy and information concentration.

That is not primitive-origin language. It is a late-regime scaling law.

Overwriting A8 immediately would blur two very different layers:

- **origin layer:** what first forces distinguishability and hierarchy at all,
- **late physical layer:** how hierarchy appears in tachyonic interface systems with concrete field-theoretic observables.

Those are not the same burden.

---

## 2. What H000 contributes that A8 does not

H000 adds the missing earlier engine:

- isolated nullity is sterile,
- isolated undifferentiated totality is sterile,
- realizability cannot stably live at either pole,
- opposed sterile poles force non-flat realizability,
- hierarchy appears through repeated unresolved distinguishability burden.

This explains **why branching pressure exists at all**. A8 does not currently do that. A8 describes a later regime where hierarchy already manifests physically.

So the right relationship is:

\[
\text{H000 (primitive bifurcation root)} \;\Longrightarrow\; \text{reiterated distinguishability / hierarchy engine} \;\Longrightarrow\; \text{A8-like tachyonic interface realization}
\]

This is a proposed dependency, not yet a proved theorem.

---

## 3. Three candidate integration strategies

### Strategy A — overwrite A8 with H000
Replace the current A8 candidate entirely with the Primitive Bifurcation Hypothesis.

**Pros**
- makes the earliest burden explicit,
- removes ambiguity about hierarchy’s deeper source.

**Cons**
- destroys a useful late-regime operational package,
- forces a premature merge between primitive and late-regime claims,
- risks losing the current tachyonic scaling predictions as a standalone falsifiable law.

**Recommendation:** reject for now.

---

### Strategy B — keep A8 and add H000 beneath it
Maintain A8 as a late-regime candidate scaling law and add H000 as a separate earlier hypothesis.

**Pros**
- preserves current A8 tests and predictions,
- keeps the primitive and late-regime layers cleanly separated,
- allows a later bridge theorem instead of a premature fusion,
- minimizes canon churn while root work is still live.

**Cons**
- temporarily creates two related hierarchy documents,
- requires careful cross-referencing to avoid confusion.

**Recommendation:** adopt now.

---

### Strategy C — rewrite A8 into a two-layer unified candidate
Create a revised A8 that begins with primitive bifurcation and culminates in tachyonic hierarchy as one later realization.

**Pros**
- elegant if it works,
- offers one unified narrative.

**Cons**
- too early right now,
- high risk of mixing theorem-grade content with conjectural bridge content,
- likely to overstate what has actually been closed.

**Recommendation:** defer until CF000 and the bridge burden mature.

## 6. What should not be changed yet

Do **not** yet rewrite the current A8 formal statement into primitive-origin language.

Reasons:

1. the current A8 document is operationally sharp in its own regime,
2. H000 is still conjectural and not yet bridged theoremically to A8,
3. mixing them now would make the document less auditable,
4. the current predictions may survive unchanged even if A8 is later demoted to a downstream realization.

## 7. Bridge theorem target

The live bridge burden can be phrased as:

### Bridge target BT1
Show that the repeated non-flat resolution of primitive bifurcation generates a hierarchy engine whose late field-theoretic realization in tachyonic metriplectic systems yields logarithmic interface depth, boundary concentration, and finite-energy admissibility of the A8 type.

This should remain a separate target until closed.

### What BT1 would need to establish

1. primitive bifurcation yields reiterated distinguishability,
2. reiterated distinguishability yields hierarchical dependence order,
3. hierarchical dependence order admits a field-level realization,
4. tachyonic pulled-front systems instantiate one such realization,
5. A8 observables can be interpreted as the late-regime signature of that earlier engine.

## 9. Risk map

### Risk 1 — premature merge
Combining H000 and A8 too early may contaminate both.

### Risk 2 — false dependency claim
It may turn out that A8 does not descend cleanly from H000 in the way currently hoped.

### Risk 3 — interpretive overreach
One might be tempted to restate A8 as already “explained” by H000 before the bridge theorem exists.

### Risk 4 — canon confusion
Without careful labeling, readers may confuse:
- primitive hierarchy origin,
- tachyonic hierarchy realization,
- and current proposal status.

---

## 10. Final recommendation

### Adopt immediately
- add H000 as a separate hypothesis artifact,
- preserve A8 as a late-regime candidate law,
- add a short primitive-origin clarification note to A8 when convenient.

### Defer
- any full overwrite of A8,
- any claim that H000 already proves A8,
- any unified axiom statement that collapses primitive origin and tachyonic realization into one theorem-grade package.

### Working one-line summary
> H000 should currently sit **beneath** A8, not replace it; A8 remains the late-regime hierarchy candidate, while H000 is the proposed earlier origin of the hierarchy engine itself.
