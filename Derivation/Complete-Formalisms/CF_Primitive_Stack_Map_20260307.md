## Ontological Questions

* What deeper primitive gives rise to a state-family in which trivial self-identity is quotiented out, so that physically meaningful difference becomes geometric and the QGT appears as the first stable tensorial shadow?

  * Attempt 1
    *  The deepest primitive in VDM is best conceived not as a graph, bond field, or already-formed geometry, but as a local state-generating substrate governed by an intrinsic evolution law. Its first ontological act is the production of admissible local state-families together with a distinction between trivial self-preservation and physically meaningful variation. Only after redundant or self-equivalent directions are removed does a true local geometry appear. The symmetric content of that surviving variation becomes distinguishability/metric structure, the antisymmetric content becomes Berry-curvature/symplectic structure, and their unification is the QGT. On this reading, the QGT is the first induced geometric object of the substrate rather than the substrate itself: it is the tensorial signature left by a deeper law that generates local states and selects which infinitesimal differences count as physically real.

    ## One paragraph
    
    The deepest layer in VDM is best understood not as support, bonds, or already-formed geometry, but as a **local state-generating substrate** with an intrinsic law of evolution. Each locality carries a **generative state-capacity**: the ability to realize a family of nearby admissible local states, not all of whose formal variations count as physically real. The first ontological operation is therefore the removal of **trivial self-identity** — redundant, self-equivalent, or pure-gauge variation — so that only genuinely meaningful local difference remains. Once that quotient is taken, local geometry appears for the first time in a rigorous form: the symmetric part of surviving variation becomes distinguishability/metric structure, the antisymmetric part becomes Berry-curvature/phase-twist structure, and together they form the QGT. On this reading, the QGT is not the substrate itself but the **first tensorial shadow** cast by a deeper law that generates local states and selects which infinitesimal differences are physically real.
    
    ## One falsifiable thesis
    
    **Falsifiable thesis:**
    If VDM is truly grounded in a deeper local state-generating substrate rather than in primitive support, bonds, or primitive J/M sectors, then the runtime must be constructible so that:
    
    1. **The QGT is induced rather than postulated**
       It must arise from local admissible state variation with the equivalent of a projector that removes trivial self-identity, rather than being inserted as an external geometric object.
    
    2. **J/M appears as a derived decomposition of evolution**
       The metriplectic split must emerge from the induced geometry/generator law, not require primitive J-objects or M-objects in the substrate.
    
    3. **Local support emerges from admissibility**
       The locality-bearing support structure must be recoverable as a derived pattern of allowed propagation/overlap/gauge relation, rather than being assumed as a primitive graph scaffold.
    
    4. **Effective link and gauge structure come later**
       Local link fields, gauge connection, plaquettes, holonomies, and nonlocal/topological observables must be derivable from the deeper state geometry rather than treated as first ontology.
    
    This thesis is falsified if any of the following turn out to be necessary:
    
    * the QGT cannot be generated from projected local state variation and must be taken as primitive,
    * the runtime cannot realize the governing law without primitive J/M ontic sectors,
    * locality/support must be fundamental and cannot be derived from deeper admissibility structure,
    * or gauge/link structure must be primitive rather than induced from overlap geometry.
    
    And here is the compact chain underneath all three:
    
    **local state-generating substrate → admissible local state-family → quotient of trivial self-identity → QGT → J/M decomposition → derived support/link/gauge/topology**
