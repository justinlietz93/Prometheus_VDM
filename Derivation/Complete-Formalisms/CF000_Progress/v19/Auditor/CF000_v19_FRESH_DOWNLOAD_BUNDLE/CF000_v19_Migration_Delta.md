# CF000 v19 — Migration Delta from v18

## Global disposition

v18 remains the correct theorem-structure baseline. v19 is a scope-control pass, not a structural rewrite.

## What v19 keeps

- v13 as governing frame.
- v15 closure of the lowest-resolution positive articulation after origin.
- The valid v18 **HRDCL / HRDAP** split.
- BEP off the lowest-resolution-articulation route.
- E1 in sharpened origin-scoped form.
- RRP downstream.

## What v19 adds

- An explicit **semantic budget** for the live HRDAP burden.
- An explicit **blocked imports** ledger for the HRDAP scope.
- An explicit **unlocked definitions if closed** ledger for HRDAP.
- A theorem-hygiene proposition forbidding proof of HRDAP by blocked or unlocked-only semantics.

## What v19 does not change

- It does not reopen WNF.
- It does not reopen the frame.
- It does not add new structural machinery.
- It does not prove HRDAP.
- It does not prove contextual noncoincidence, apartness, RRP, locality, frontier, coherence, or pre-carrier support.

## Net dependency effect

### v18 effective order
1. E1
2. lowest-resolution invariant-bearing differentiated degree after origin
3. HRDCL — character of a genuinely higher-resolution differentiated degree
4. HRDAP — admission of a genuinely higher-resolution differentiated degree
5. contextual noncoincidence / RRP downstream

### v19 effective order
1. E1
2. lowest-resolution invariant-bearing differentiated degree after origin
3. HRDCL — character of a genuinely higher-resolution differentiated degree
4. **HRDAP under semantic budget**
5. contextual noncoincidence / apartness / RRP only after unlocked semantics become live

## Interpretation

v19 turns the dependency ladder into an operational guardrail. The main win is not a new theorem. It is a cleaner proof environment that makes semantic circularity visible before it can masquerade as progress.
