# CF000 v12 — Contamination Self-Audit
Date: 2026-03-13  
Focus: WNF / internal noncoincidence pass

---

## Audit target

Check whether the v12 WNF pass smuggles later structure into the first positive witness layer.

---

## 1. Flatness inflation risk

**Risk:** define flatness so strongly that witness structure, multiplicity, or apartness are already hidden inside $\neg\mathrm{Flat}$.

**v12 verdict:** mitigated, not eliminated.

**Reason:** flatness remains an internal structural condition only. The manuscript explicitly proves that positive witness does **not** follow from PB1–PB5 and E1 alone.

---

## 2. Witness inflation risk

**Risk:** define internal noncoincidence so strongly that it already means “there are two things.”

**v12 verdict:** mitigated.

**Reason:** WNF introduces only positive witness structure. It does not yet decompose witness into two internal determinations. Multiplicity remains a later burden under DWP.

---

## 3. Apartness smuggling risk

**Risk:** treat witness structure as already apartness.

**v12 verdict:** mitigated.

**Reason:** apartness is explicitly blocked until multiplicity closes. The manuscript does not define apartness until after DWP-level decomposition.

---

## 4. Tree/graph contamination risk

**Risk:** turn recursive refinement into hidden ancestry, branching, or depth language.

**v12 verdict:** mitigated, still live.

**Reason:** RRP is named but not overformalized. No branch map, tree, frontier, or ancestry object is inserted in this pass.

---

## 5. Witness-domain overpackaging risk

**Risk:** the auxiliary witness domain $\mathcal W$ may be read as a primitive ontology of entities rather than as a logical certificate layer.

**v12 verdict:** still live.

**Reason:** the manuscript states that witnesses are not thereby objects in the world. This must remain enforced in later passes.

---

## 6. Logical-domain plurality risk

**Risk:** the logical discourse domain $\mathcal C$ may silently do ontological work and prepay plurality.

**v12 verdict:** still live.

**Reason:** the manuscript keeps $\mathcal C$ as logical discourse only, but later passes must continue auditing this.

---

## 7. Status discipline audit

Required status language for major claims:
- Proved
- Requires one new explicit principle
- Not proved
- Blocked

**v12 verdict:** satisfied in the revised manuscript.

---

## 8. Overall contamination verdict

v12 is cleaner than the prior line at the exact point that mattered. The first positive bridge is no longer allowed to inflate directly into plurality. The remaining contamination pressure is concentrated at:

1. witness-domain interpretation,
2. witness-to-multiplicity decomposition,
3. recursive refinement formalization.

That is the right place for the danger to live. It means the manuscript has stopped lying about where the real tooth is.
