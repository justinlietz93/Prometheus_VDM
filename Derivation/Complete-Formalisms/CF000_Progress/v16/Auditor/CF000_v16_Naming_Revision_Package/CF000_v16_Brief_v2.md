# CF000 v16 Brief — Dependency-Safe Naming Update

## Settled baseline
- v13 remains the governing bifurcation frame.
- v15 remains the correct closure of the first positive articulation.
- v16's split between **character** and **availability** for the next articulation burden is real and should be preserved.
- BEP stays off the first-articulation route.
- E1 stays origin-scoped.

## What is not settled
The theorem-spine vocabulary is still not clean enough.

Two problems remain:
- spatial wording such as **widening**
- sequence-heavy wording such as **first** when dependence-ranked language is more accurate

## What the next pass should do
Keep the valid v16 split, but recast it in pre-geometric, dependence-ranked language:

- the invariant is already there at every admissible degree,
- stricter degrees are more articulated inhabitations of the same invariant,
- stricter degrees clarify prior ones,
- and the next live burden is whether a **stricter differentiated degree** is available, not whether anything “widens.”

## Preferred direction
Use language like:
- lowest-resolution invariant-bearing differentiated degree after origin
- stricter differentiated degree
- higher-resolution articulation
- additional determination
- clarification of prior degree

Avoid both spatial language and sequence-implying wording in the theorem spine.
