I think v16 made a real improvement, but it still needs one more cleanup pass at the language level.

The good part of v16 should stay:
- v13 remains the governing frame,
- v15 first positive articulation remains closed,
- the old WNF witness bottleneck stays retired,
- and the split between **character** and **availability** for the next articulation burden is legitimate.

So the next pass should **not** undo that split.

However, the theorem-spine language still needs tightening in two ways:

1. it still uses some physical / spatially suggestive vocabulary, and  
2. it still uses sequence-heavy words like **first** in places where the doctrine is really about **shared resolution dependency**, not chronological order.

The doctrine note now makes the cleaner rule explicit:

- the invariant is already present at every admissible degree,
- higher degrees do not inherit a new invariant,
- they are more articulated inhabitations of the same invariant,
- a stricter degree clarifies the prior one,
- once the stricter degree is inhabited, the prior degree is no longer sufficient as the sole determination of the state,
- and the active ambiguity shifts to the newest frontier.

That is the correct root picture.

## Required task

Keep the real v16 split, but recast it completely in **pre-geometric, dependence-ranked language**.

The next burden should no longer be phrased with spatial words like “widening,” and it should also avoid sequence-heavy labels like “first” when dependence language is more accurate.

Instead, ask:

> Does the **lowest-resolution invariant-bearing differentiated degree after origin** admit a genuinely **stricter differentiated degree** under the same invariant?

Then separate the two burdens this way:

### 1. Character burden
If such a genuinely stricter degree exists, what must that imply structurally?

The likely direction is:
- the prior degree is no longer sufficient as sole determination,
- an additional internal determination is present,
- but apartness, nodehood, locality, and metric structure still remain unearned.

### 2. Availability burden
Does the current package actually prove that such a genuinely stricter degree exists?

If not, isolate the exact minimal extra principle needed.

## Hard constraints

1. Keep **v13** frozen as the governing frame.
2. Keep **v15** frozen as the closure of the first positive articulation.
3. Preserve the valid **v16 character / availability split**.
4. Retire spatial vocabulary from the theorem spine:
   - no widening
   - no spreading
   - no branching
   - no expansion
   - no lateral language
5. Retire sequence-heavy wording when dependence language is sufficient:
   - avoid “first” if “lowest-resolution ... after origin” is more accurate
   - avoid “next” if “stricter” or “higher-resolution” is more accurate
6. Do not import time, causality, locality, metric, graph, tree, carrier, or completed boundaries.
7. Do not let a stricter degree quietly become geometric dimension.
8. Do not let additional determination quietly become apartness.
9. Keep the language sparse, theorem-grade, and structurally literal.

## Preferred naming direction

Please use non-spatial and non-sequential terms such as:
- **lowest-resolution invariant-bearing differentiated degree after origin**
- **stricter differentiated degree**
- **higher-resolution articulation**
- **additional determination**
- **clarification of prior degree**

If you rename the v16 split, keep it minimal and justified. Do not proliferate labels unless each label tracks a real dependency difference.
