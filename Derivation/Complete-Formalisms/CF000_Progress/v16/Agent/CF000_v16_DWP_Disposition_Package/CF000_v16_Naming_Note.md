
# CF000 v16 — Naming Note

## Verdict

The theorem-spine phrase **"distinction of change"** is retired in v16.

The adopted structural term is:

> **first invariant-bearing differentiated degree**

## Why the term changed

The older phrase was useful as a correction against witness inflation, but it remained semantically risky because it could suggest:

- time,
- event sequence,
- process,
- or causality.

The v16 term is safer because it says exactly what the theorem closes:

- the same unresolved primitive invariant is still present,
- it is now carried in a differentiated articulation,
- and this is the first positive articulated degree earned on the root route.

## What the new term does not mean

It does **not** mean:

- manifold differentiability,
- smoothness,
- derivative structure,
- temporal change,
- or causal transition.

It means only a primitive articulated degree of invariant carriage.

## Practical rule

In the manuscript body, use **first invariant-bearing differentiated degree** for theorem statements and dependency order.

The older phrase may appear only in migration notes or historical crosswalk remarks.
