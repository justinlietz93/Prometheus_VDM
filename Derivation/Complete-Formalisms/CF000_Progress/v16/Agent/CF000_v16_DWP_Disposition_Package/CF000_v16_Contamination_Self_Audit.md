
# CF000 v16 — Contamination Self-Audit

## Purpose

This audit checks whether the v16 pass stayed inside the AC invariant discipline while performing the narrower DWP disposition and naming-tightening pass.

## Pass verdict

**Overall status:** Acceptable with named risks.

v16 keeps the v13 frame and the v15 first-articulation closure intact, retires the risky theorem-spine phrase **"distinction of change"**, and splits coarse DWP into proved widening character plus open widening availability.

## Main risks checked

### 1. Reopening the frame
**Status:** Not present.

The pass does not reopen the v13 one-origin bifurcation frame and does not restore the v14 witness split.

### 2. Using temporal language for the first articulation
**Status:** Reduced.

The manuscript now uses **first invariant-bearing differentiated degree** instead of **"distinction of change"** in the theorem spine.

### 3. Letting DWP smuggle in ready-made plurality
**Status:** Reduced but still active.

v16 explicitly proves only the **character** of genuine widening and leaves **availability** open as WAP.

### 4. Importing apartness or nodehood into widening
**Status:** Guarded.

Multiplicity is still weakly internal and does not yet assume apartness, graph structure, locality, or metric separation.

### 5. Treating global non-discharge as automatic widening
**Status:** Guarded.

The manuscript explicitly proves that widening availability does not yet follow from PB1–PB5 and E1.

### 6. Letting local settlement sound like global resolution
**Status:** Guarded.

The local/global split remains explicit and continues to constrain later interpretation.

## Residual concerns

1. The schematic shorthand for “genuine widening” is still prose-level rather than fully object-level formalized. That is acceptable for this pass, but later closure may require a cleaner internal relation if WAP is attacked directly.
2. The phrase **"increase in complexity"** comes from the AC invariant discipline and is being used structurally, not quantitatively. Later drafts should keep that distinction explicit.
3. WAP will be the next place where future-concept borrowing is tempting. The next pass must resist importing recursion, locality, or nodehood prematurely.

## Conclusion

v16 is tighter than v15. The dominant remaining risk is no longer temporal language or coarse DWP inflation; it is premature proof of widening availability.
