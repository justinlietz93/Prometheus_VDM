# CF000 v14 Updated Open-Burden Inventory

Date: 2026-03-14

## Governing frame

v13 remains the baseline. The active burdens are still best understood as later articulations of one globally undischarged primitive bifurcation invariant.

## Active articulation chain

### 1. E1 — realizable origin existence burden
Question: must a realizable origin-bearing articulation exist, or must that remain explicit?

Why it matters: without E1, the articulation chain is still vacuous.

### 2. Strong WNF — witness-lift burden
Question: once weak non-self-coincidence exists under the invariant, must it lift to a positive theorem-usable internal witness?

Why it matters: this is the true remaining first positive articulation burden.

### 3. DWP — first plurality-bearing articulation burden
Question: must witnessed internal noncoincidence articulate into plurality-bearing form?

Why it matters: without DWP, multiplicity remains unearned and apartness stays blocked.

### 4. RRP — higher-resolution restatement burden
Question: when local articulation remains unsettled, must the same invariant reappear at a more articulated level?

Why it matters: without RRP, the manuscript cannot honestly move toward recursive refinement or hierarchy.

### 5. WEP — truncation / frontier burden
Question: does repeated articulation eventually force some well-founded exhaustion or truncation stage?

Why it matters: without WEP or an equivalent result, frontier/boundary-type structure stays speculative.

## Parked but still relevant

### BEP — root exhaustion burden
Question: do the two distinguished poles exhaust all admissible flat sterile origin extremes?

Why it matters: this still matters for stronger root-classification closure, but it is no longer a live blocker for the WNF route.

## Important warning

The open burdens must not be narrated as separate engines. Strong WNF, DWP, and RRP are still only sharper formal statements of the same invariant burden at later articulation stages.

## Still downstream and blocked

- apartness before multiplicity,
- hierarchy before RRP,
- locality before truncation / later structure,
- coherence before locality,
- pre-carrier bridge before coherence,
- differentiability before pre-carrier closure.
