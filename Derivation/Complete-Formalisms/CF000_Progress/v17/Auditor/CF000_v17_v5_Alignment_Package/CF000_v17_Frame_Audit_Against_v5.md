# CF000 v17 Frame Audit Against v5 Doctrine

## Verdict

**v17 is a real improvement and should be treated as the new baseline for the next pass.**
But it is **not yet fully aligned** with the newer v5 doctrine note.

The good part is real:
- v13 frame stays frozen
- v15 closure stays closed
- v16 split stays valid
- spatial theorem-spine wording like **widening** is retired
- the next live bite is narrower and more exact than before

So this is **not** fake progress.

## What v17 gets right

- It correctly keeps the one-origin bifurcation frame frozen.
- It correctly preserves the lowest-resolution positive articulation closure.
- It correctly preserves the split between:
  1. character of a stricter differentiated degree
  2. admission/proof of such a stricter differentiated degree
- It correctly blocks apartness, locality, metric structure, boundaries, and recursion from being smuggled in early.
- It correctly identifies the next real bottleneck as the admission of a genuinely stricter differentiated degree.

## What v17 still carries from pre-v5 wording

v17 is cleaner than v16, but it still uses several terms that the newer v5 doctrine now treats as slippery:

### 1. "same"
v17 repeatedly says:
- **same invariant**
- **same unresolved burden**

Conceptually this is understandable, but v5 is right to warn that **same** invites a comparison relation that may not yet be earned cleanly at the theorem-spine layer.

### 2. "prior"
v17 repeatedly says:
- **prior degree**
- **clarification of the prior degree**

This is cleaner than chronological language like "first/next," but it still leans sequence-shaped.  
The safer v5 direction is:
- **lower-resolution degree**
- **higher-resolution degree**

### 3. "availability"
v17 names the open tooth:
- **Stricter-Degree Availability Principle**

This is much better than widening, but **availability** still sounds like a pre-given menu of options or external relation.  
The safer v5 direction is to frame the burden around **admission** or **closure of increased resolution through admissible degrees of invariant-bearing articulation**.

### 4. "after origin"
This phrase is probably acceptable if carefully anchored, but it still carries a weak sequence flavor. It should be used sparingly and only where it really helps scope the lowest-resolution articulation.

## Main structural concern

The remaining problem is **not** bad theorem structure.

The remaining problem is **vocabulary drift at the theorem spine**:
- comparison drift
- sequence drift
- menu/relation drift

So v17 is now very close.  
The next pass should not reopen the split or add new machinery.  
It should just align the spine with the stricter v5 doctrine.

## Recommended theorem-spine direction

The safest new center of gravity is:

> **increased resolution through admissible degrees of invariant-bearing articulation**

This is cleaner than:
- widening
- same
- prior
- availability

And it better matches the v5 doctrine.

## Recommended minimal cleanup

Keep the v17 structure, but rename and recast:

- **same invariant** -> avoid where possible; use **invariant-bearing articulation** without comparison-heavy phrasing
- **prior degree** -> **lower-resolution degree**
- **stricter degree** -> may remain if used sparingly, but **higher-resolution degree** is safer
- **availability** -> **admission** or **closure**
- **SDAP** -> something like:
  - **Higher-Resolution Degree Admission Principle**
  - or another equally sparse non-relational name

## Pass/Fail judgment

**Pass as theorem-structure improvement.**  
**Fail only as final doctrine-aligned wording.**

## Bottom line

v17 did the hard part.
v18 should be a language-hygiene alignment pass, not a structural rewrite.
