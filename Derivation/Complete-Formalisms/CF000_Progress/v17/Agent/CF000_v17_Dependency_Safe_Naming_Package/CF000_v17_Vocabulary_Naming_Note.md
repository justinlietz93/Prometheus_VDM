# CF000 v17 — Vocabulary / Naming Note

## Purpose

v17 retires the remaining theorem-spine wording that risked dragging the manuscript toward spatial or chronological interpretation too early.

## Retired wording

The following are retired from the theorem spine:

- **widening**
- other lateral or spatially suggestive variants of the same idea
- sequence-heavy phrasing where dependence-ranked wording is more accurate

## Adopted wording

The theorem spine now prefers:

- **lowest-resolution invariant-bearing differentiated degree after origin**
- **genuinely stricter differentiated degree**
- **higher-resolution articulation**
- **additional internal determination**
- **clarification of the prior degree**

## Why this matters

These replacements are not cosmetic.

They enforce the doctrinal rule that:

- the invariant is already present at every admissible degree,
- stricter degrees do not inherit a new invariant,
- stricter degrees are more articulated inhabitations of the same invariant,
- and the live burden is availability and character of further articulation, not spatial widening or temporal sequence.
