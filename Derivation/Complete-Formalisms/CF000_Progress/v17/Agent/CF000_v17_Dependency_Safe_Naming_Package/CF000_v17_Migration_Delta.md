# CF000 v17 — Migration Delta from v16

## Global disposition

v16 is retained as the correct baseline for the character / availability split, but not as the cleanest statement of that split.

## What v17 removes

- spatial theorem-spine wording such as **widening**
- sequence-heavy theorem-spine wording where dependence-ranked language is more accurate

## What v17 keeps

- v13 as governing frame
- v15 closure of the lowest-resolution positive articulation after origin
- v16 character / availability split
- BEP off the lowest-resolution-articulation route
- E1 in sharpened origin-scoped form
- RRP downstream

## What v17 adds

- explicit doctrine that the invariant is already present at every admissible degree
- explicit doctrine that stricter degrees clarify prior ones rather than inheriting a new invariant
- explicit character theorem phrased as **additional internal determination** rather than spatial widening

## Net dependency effect

### v16 effective order
1. E1
2. lowest-resolution invariant-bearing differentiated degree after origin
3. character of widening
4. availability of widening
5. actual multiplicity / apartness / RRP downstream

### v17 effective order
1. E1
2. lowest-resolution invariant-bearing differentiated degree after origin
3. SDCL — character of a genuinely stricter differentiated degree
4. SDAP — availability of a genuinely stricter differentiated degree
5. contextual apartness / RRP downstream

## Interpretation

v17 does not add new machinery. It removes packaging contamination so the active bite is stated at the correct primitive layer.
