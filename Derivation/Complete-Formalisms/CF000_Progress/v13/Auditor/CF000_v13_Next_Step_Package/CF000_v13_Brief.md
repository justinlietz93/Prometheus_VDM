# CF000 v13 Brief

## Bottom line

**v13 is a real frame correction and should become the new baseline.**

It successfully replaces the old modular-burden feel with the stronger governing claim that the primitive origin is one condition carrying an unresolved pole-opposition that is never globally discharged. On that frame, WNF, DWP, and RRP are now presented as successive articulations of one invariant rather than as mostly separate engines.

## What landed

- The manuscript now treats the primitive tension as **derived**, not merely assumed.
- The origin is now explicitly **one primitive condition**, not two externally separated things.
- The no-prior-framework discipline is now explicit: no primitive space, time, locality, direction, metric, or value background.
- Local stability is now consistently framed as **local settlement**, not global resolution.
- WNF is now framed as the **first positive articulation** of the invariant.
- DWP is now framed as the **first plurality-bearing articulation** of the invariant.
- RRP is now framed as the **higher-resolution restatement** of the same invariant.

## What remains open

v13 is **not** a closure pass. It still leaves the key burdens open:

- **BEP** — bipolar exhaustion
- **E1** — realizable existence
- **WNF** — first positive articulation
- **DWP** — first plurality-bearing articulation
- **RRP** — higher-resolution restatement

That is acceptable. The frame rewrite was the right move.

## What not to do next

Do **not** spend the next pass doing another global rewrite.
Do **not** reopen the frame unless a theorem really forces it.
Do **not** let the agent drift back into treating WNF, DWP, and RRP as disconnected modules.

## What to do next

Freeze v13 as the architectural baseline.

Then attack **WNF first**, but under the corrected invariant frame. The next pass should answer one exact question:

> Is WNF derivable from the current root package plus the now-explicit one-origin invariant discipline, or does it still require one new explicit principle?

That pass should also force a crisp dependency verdict on **BEP** and **E1**:

- Is **BEP** actually needed for the WNF route, or only for a stronger classification claim?
- Is **E1** still an explicit existence axiom, or can any weaker/cleaner existential condition be isolated?

## Practical recommendation

The next pass should be a **WNF disposition pass**, not another architecture pass.

Its job is to decide whether:
1. WNF can now be proved from the revised root frame,
2. WNF still cannot be proved and needs a minimal new principle,
3. or the manuscript must split the question into a weaker lemma and a stronger witness principle.

DWP and RRP should stay downstream in that pass unless the WNF analysis forces a change in their wording.
