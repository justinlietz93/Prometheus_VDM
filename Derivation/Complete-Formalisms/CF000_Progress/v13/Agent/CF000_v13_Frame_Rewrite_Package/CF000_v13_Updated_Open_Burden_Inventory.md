# CF000 v13 Updated Open-Burden Inventory

Date: 2026-03-14

## Governing frame

The active burdens are no longer best described as isolated modules. They are the next formal articulations of one globally undischarged primitive bifurcation invariant.

## Active articulation chain

### 1. BEP — root exhaustion burden
Question: do the two distinguished poles exhaust all admissible flat sterile origin extremes, or must that remain an explicit principle?

Why it matters: without BEP, the claimed forced origin architecture remains underdetermined at the classification step.

### 2. E1 — realizable existence burden
Question: is realizable non-vacuous origin existence derivable, or must it remain explicit?

Why it matters: without E1, the universal root laws remain vacuous.

### 3. WNF — first positive articulation burden
Question: once differentiated articulation exists under the invariant, must some positive internal noncoincidence be present?

Why it matters: this is the first move from mere negative anti-flatness to positive articulated structure.

### 4. DWP — first plurality-bearing articulation burden
Question: must internal noncoincidence articulate into plurality-bearing form?

Why it matters: without DWP, multiplicity remains unearned and apartness stays blocked.

### 5. RRP — higher-resolution restatement burden
Question: when local articulation remains unsettled, must the same invariant reappear at a more articulated level?

Why it matters: without RRP, the manuscript cannot honestly move toward recursive refinement or hierarchy.

### 6. WEP — truncation / frontier burden
Question: does repeated articulation eventually force some well-founded exhaustion or truncation stage?

Why it matters: without WEP or an equivalent result, frontier/boundary-type structure stays speculative.

## Important warning

The open burdens must not be narrated as separate engines. Each is only a sharper formal statement of the same invariant burden at a later articulation stage.
