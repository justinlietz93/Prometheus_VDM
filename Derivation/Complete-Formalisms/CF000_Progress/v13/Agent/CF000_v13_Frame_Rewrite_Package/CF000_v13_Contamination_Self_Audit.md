# CF000 v13 Contamination Self-Audit

Date: 2026-03-14  
Scope: Frame rewrite over the full manuscript

## Verdict

v13 fixes the governing frame substantially better than v12. The primitive bifurcation invariant now actually governs the manuscript rather than appearing as a decorative preface. The main remaining risk is no longer local theorem hygiene; it is whether future passes will quietly collapse back into modular burden talk and forget that WNF, DWP, and RRP are expressions of one still-live invariant.

## What changed correctly

- The origin is now stated explicitly as **one primitive condition**, not two externally separated things.
- The primitive tension is now stated explicitly as **derived**, not posited.
- The manuscript now states explicitly that there is initially **no prior space, time, location, direction, metric, locality, or value framework** in which the poles could stand apart.
- The unresolved pole-opposition is now stated explicitly as the **global invariant** of the system.
- The manuscript now states explicitly that the invariant is **never globally discharged**.
- Local stability is now consistently framed as **local settlement** rather than global resolution.
- WNF is rewritten as the **first positive articulation** of the invariant.
- DWP is rewritten as the **first plurality-bearing articulation** of the invariant.
- RRP is rewritten as the **higher-resolution restatement** of the same invariant rather than optional reactivation.

## Top remaining contamination risks

### 1. Externalizing the poles
Even with the rewrite, readers may still picture two primitive items facing each other. That would destroy the one-origin doctrine. Future passes must keep repeating that the poles are logically distinct but primitively undifferentiated in being.

### 2. Treating WNF as static
There is still a risk that WNF gets read as “attach one witness and move on.” That is wrong. WNF must remain the first positive manifestation of a burden that has not gone away.

### 3. Treating DWP as arbitrary plurality glue
If DWP is written as a generic decomposability rule rather than as the next articulation of the same invariant, plurality will feel imported rather than earned.

### 4. Treating RRP as optional reactivation
RRP must never read as “turn the engine back on later.” The engine never turned off. RRP is the explicit statement that the same invariant reappears when prior articulation remains locally unsettled.

### 5. Letting local settlement sound final
Any words like “stabilized,” “coherent,” “resolved,” or “settled” must be checked for whether they refer only to local settlement. The manuscript still cannot license global final resolution.

## What v13 still does not fix

- It does not prove BEP.
- It does not prove E1.
- It does not prove WNF.
- It does not prove DWP.
- It does not prove RRP.
- It does not close locality, frontier, coherence, pre-carrier support, or differentiability.

That is acceptable. The rewrite target was governing architecture, not false closure.

## Pass/Fail Judgment

**Pass as governing-frame correction.**  
**Fail, correctly, as Completed Formalism.**
