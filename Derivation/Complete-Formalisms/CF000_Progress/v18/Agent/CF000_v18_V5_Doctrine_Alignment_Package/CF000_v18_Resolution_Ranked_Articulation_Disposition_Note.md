# CF000 v18 — Resolution-Ranked Articulation Disposition Note

## Verdict

v18 keeps the valid v17 structure but aligns the theorem spine to the stricter v5 doctrine language.

The live articulation burden is now stated this way:

> Does the current root package close **increased resolution through admissible degrees of invariant-bearing articulation**?

That burden remains split into two parts:

1. **HRDCL — Higher-Resolution Degree Character Lemma**  
   Closed. If a lowest-resolution invariant-bearing differentiated degree after origin admits a genuinely higher-resolution differentiated degree under the undischarged primitive invariant, then the lower-resolution degree is no longer sufficient as the sole determination of the state and an additional internal determination is present.

2. **HRDAP — Higher-Resolution Degree Admission Principle**  
   Still open. The current package does not yet prove that such a genuinely higher-resolution differentiated degree is admitted by the present package.

## Net effect

- v13 frame stays frozen.
- v15 closure of the lowest-resolution positive articulation after origin stays closed.
- The valid v17 character/admission split stays valid.
- Comparison-heavy theorem-spine wording is reduced.
- Sequence-shaped theorem-spine wording is reduced.
- Menu/relation drift from **availability** is retired in favor of **admission** or **closure**.
- The next real bite remains **HRDAP**, not witness bookkeeping, not BEP, and not a new frame rewrite.
