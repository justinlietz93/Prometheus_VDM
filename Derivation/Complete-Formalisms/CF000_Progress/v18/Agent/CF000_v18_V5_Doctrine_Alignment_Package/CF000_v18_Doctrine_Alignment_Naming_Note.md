# CF000 v18 — Doctrine-Alignment Naming Note

## Purpose

v18 does not reopen the structure. It aligns the already-valid v17 theorem spine to the stricter v5 doctrine language.

## Retired wording

The following are retired from the theorem spine:

- **same** where it invites unearned direct comparison,
- **prior** where it invites sequence-shaped reading,
- **availability** where it sounds like a pre-given menu or external relation.

## Adopted wording

The theorem spine now prefers:

- **lower-resolution degree**
- **higher-resolution degree**
- **admission** or **closure**
- **increased resolution through admissible degrees of invariant-bearing articulation**
- **invariant-bearing articulation under the undischarged primitive invariant**

## Why this matters

These replacements are not cosmetic.

They enforce the doctrinal rule that:

- the invariant is already present at every admissible degree,
- higher-resolution degrees do not acquire a fresh invariant,
- lower-resolution degrees are clarified rather than erased,
- and the live burden is admission and character of increased resolution, not a menu of options, not temporal succession, and not spatial spread.
