# CF000 v18 — Migration Delta from v17

## Global disposition

v17 is retained as the correct baseline for theorem structure, but not as the cleanest statement of that structure under the stricter v5 doctrine.

## What v18 removes

- comparison-heavy theorem-spine wording such as **same** where it invites unearned direct comparison
- sequence-shaped theorem-spine wording such as **prior** where resolution-ranked wording is more accurate
- menu/relation wording such as **availability** where **admission** or **closure** is theorem-cleaner

## What v18 keeps

- v13 as governing frame
- v15 closure of the lowest-resolution positive articulation after origin
- the valid v17 character / admission split
- BEP off the lowest-resolution-articulation route
- E1 in sharpened origin-scoped form
- RRP downstream

## What v18 adds

- explicit alignment to the doctrine phrase **increased resolution through admissible degrees of invariant-bearing articulation**
- explicit replacement of **prior degree** with **lower-resolution degree**
- explicit replacement of **stricter degree** with **higher-resolution degree** where theorem-cleaner
- explicit replacement of **availability** with **admission** or **closure**

## Net dependency effect

### v17 effective order
1. E1
2. lowest-resolution invariant-bearing differentiated degree after origin
3. SDCL — character of a genuinely stricter differentiated degree
4. SDAP — admission of a genuinely stricter differentiated degree
5. contextual apartness / RRP downstream

### v18 effective order
1. E1
2. lowest-resolution invariant-bearing differentiated degree after origin
3. HRDCL — character of a genuinely higher-resolution differentiated degree
4. HRDAP — admission of a genuinely higher-resolution differentiated degree
5. contextual apartness / RRP downstream

## Interpretation

v18 does not add new machinery. It removes the remaining doctrine drift so the active bite is stated at the correct primitive layer in v5-safe language.
