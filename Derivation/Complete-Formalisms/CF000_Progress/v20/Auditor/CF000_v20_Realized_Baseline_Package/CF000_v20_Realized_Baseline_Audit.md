# CF000 v20 Audit — Realized Baseline / Frontier Correction

## Core correction

A newly closed baseline must be treated as:

1. a **realized degree of invariant admissibility**, and
2. the **new live frontier** at which the invariant must still be present.

The open question is **not** whether the realized baseline counts as a realized degree.
The open question is whether that realized degree exposes a **further** admissible degree.

## Why this matters

The invariant axiom does not allow a realized baseline to become a terminal resting point.

If the origin could not discharge unresolved self-opposition, then no later articulation may be treated as globally discharged either.

So every legitimate closure has two simultaneous statuses:

- **closed** relative to the earlier scaffold
- **open** as the newest frontier of invariant-bearing articulation

Any pass that speaks as though a new baseline might simply stop unless some external restart principle appears is off-frame.

## What v20 got right

- v13 frame remains frozen
- v15 closure remains closed
- semantic-budget hygiene is now operational
- the proof fork around HRDAP is sharply isolated

## What still needs correction

The next pass must not let the manuscript sound as though:
- the current realized baseline is somehow still “not yet admitted,” or
- failure to prove the further degree means the current degree is not real.

That is the framing error.

## Correct next question

Not:
- is the current baseline really a realized degree?

But:
- given that the current baseline **is** a realized degree, how is the invariant present there, and what further admissible articulation does that require?

## Bottom line

The next pass should preserve v20's guardrails but explicitly add:

> every newly closed baseline is already a realized degree of invariant admissibility, and the live burden always sits at the newly exposed frontier, not back on the closed status of the realized degree.
