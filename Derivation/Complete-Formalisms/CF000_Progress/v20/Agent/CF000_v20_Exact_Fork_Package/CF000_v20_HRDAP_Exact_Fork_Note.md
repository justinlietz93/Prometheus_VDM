# CF000 v20 — HRDAP Exact-Fork Note

## Global disposition

v19 remains the correct theorem-hygiene baseline. v20 is a narrow continuation pass, not a frame rewrite.

- v13 as governing frame.
- v15 closure of the lowest-resolution positive articulation after origin.
- The valid HRDCL / HRDAP split.

## What v20 adds

- An explicit no-go stating that the present package does **not** license the lowest-resolution degree as globally complete.
- An explicit exact-fork disposition:
  1. either close HRDAP under the current semantic budget, or
  2. state and justify an explicit obstruction/no-go principle.
- Replacement of residual “live frontier” wording with dependence-safe phrasing such as “highest admitted articulation degree” and “live insufficiency.”

## What v20 does not change

- It does not prove HRDAP.
- It does not prove contextual noncoincidence or apartness.
- It does not prove RRP.
- It does not move locality, frontier, coherence, pre-carrier support, or differentiability forward.

## Net effect

v20 makes one subtle but important correction: failure to prove global completeness of the lowest-resolution degree is **not** the same as proof that a higher-resolution degree is admitted. The manuscript now says that explicitly.
