# CF000 v20 — Migration Delta from v19

## Global disposition

v19 remains the correct theorem-hygiene baseline. v20 is an exact-fork / non-finality guardrail pass, not a structural rewrite.

## What v20 keeps

- v13 as governing frame.
- v15 closure of the lowest-resolution positive articulation after origin.
- The valid HRDCL / HRDAP split.
- The v19 semantic budget around HRDAP.
- BEP off the lowest-resolution-articulation route.
- E1 in sharpened origin-scoped form.
- RRP downstream.

## What v20 adds

- A no-go stating that global completeness of the lowest-resolution degree does not follow from the present package.
- An exact-fork disposition:
  1. close HRDAP under the current semantic budget, or
  2. introduce and justify an explicit obstruction/no-go principle.
- Dependence-safe replacement of residual “live frontier” language.

## What v20 does not change

- It does not prove HRDAP.
- It does not prove contextual noncoincidence, apartness, RRP, locality, frontier, coherence, or pre-carrier support.
- It does not add new structural machinery.

## Net dependency effect

### v19 effective order
1. E1
2. lowest-resolution invariant-bearing differentiated degree after origin
3. HRDCL — character of a genuinely higher-resolution differentiated degree
4. HRDAP under semantic budget
5. contextual noncoincidence / apartness / RRP only after unlocked semantics become live

### v20 effective order
1. E1
2. lowest-resolution invariant-bearing differentiated degree after origin
3. HRDCL — character of a genuinely higher-resolution differentiated degree
4. no-go on global completeness of the lowest-resolution degree
5. HRDAP under semantic budget **or** explicit obstruction/no-go principle
6. contextual noncoincidence / apartness / RRP only after admission actually closes

## Interpretation

v20 does not make admission easier. It makes the fork more exact by preventing a common framing error: treating lack of closure at the lower degree as if that were already proof of admission at a stricter degree.
