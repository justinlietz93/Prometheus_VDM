## Naming correction
- [ ] Replace or tighten **"distinction of change"** if it risks suggesting time, process, or causality.

  - **first distinguished degree of variation**
  - **first invariant-bearing differentiated degree**

- [ ] Ask only: when does a distinguished differentiated degree require more than one internally distinct determination?
