# CF000 v15 — Contamination Self-Audit

## Purpose

This audit checks whether the v15 pass stayed inside the AC invariant discipline and avoided reintroducing future-stage structure.

## Pass verdict

**Overall status:** Acceptable with named risks.

The v15 pass simplified the first positive articulation burden instead of adding new abstract layers. It replaced the v14 witness split with direct **distinction of change** and left plurality, apartness, recursive refinement, truncation, locality, and carrier burden downstream.

## Main risks checked

### 1. Externalizing the poles
**Status:** Not present in the main rewrite.

The manuscript continues to treat the origin as one primitive condition with internally unreconcilable poles, not two pre-existing objects.

### 2. Using future concepts to define present change
**Status:** Reduced.

The first positive articulation is no longer phrased through extra witness usability language. It is stated directly as new differentiated carriage of the invariant.

### 3. Treating distinction as multiplicity
**Status:** Guarded but still risky.

The manuscript explicitly proves that distinction of change alone does not force multiplicity. This is the main anti-smuggling guard of v15.

### 4. Reading degree language as smoothness
**Status:** Guarded.

The manuscript now explicitly states that the relevant degree is a primitive articulated degree of invariant carriage, not manifold differentiability.

### 5. Letting local settlement sound like global resolution
**Status:** Guarded.

The local/global split remains explicit.

### 6. Treating RRP as optional reactivation
**Status:** Guarded.

RRP remains phrased as higher-resolution restatement of the same invariant, not as a fresh engine.

## Residual concerns

1. The notation $\mathrm{Inv}(x)$ still carries architectural weight and could tempt later drafts to overread it as an ontology rather than a manuscript-level shorthand.
2. The phrase “degree of differentiability” remains semantically dangerous because it naturally evokes later smoothness notions.
3. DWP wording will need care in the next pass so that plurality does not appear to pop out by fiat.

## Conclusion

v15 is cleaner than v14 on the first-positive-articulation route. The dominant remaining risk is no longer witness inflation; it is premature plurality inflation in the next pass.
