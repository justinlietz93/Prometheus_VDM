# CF000 v15 — Distinction of Change Disposition

## Verdict

**Outcome A.** The direct criterion closes the first positive articulation bottleneck.

A root-level distinction already exists when the unresolved primitive invariant is carried in a new degree of differentiability. That changed differentiated degree is itself the first real distinction. No separate witness-lift layer is required on the main dependency path to state or prove that first change.

## What changed from v14

v14 split the old WNF burden into:

- a weak layer already closed, and
- a strong witness-lift layer still open.

v15 keeps the hygiene benefit of that split as a historical warning, but removes it from the live dependency spine. The split does not mark a necessary root-level theorem gap once the AC invariant rule is adopted.

## Exact dependency verdict

- **BEP:** not required for the first-distinction route.
- **E1:** still the correct explicit premise, in sharpened origin-scoped form.
- **First positive articulation:** closes directly as **distinction of change** once E1 is admitted.
- **DWP:** remains the next live burden.
- **RRP:** remains downstream of distinction and plurality.

## Why the direct criterion wins

The corrected question is not:

- what later object certifies the first change?

It is:

- what changed in the invariant itself?

The answer is:

- the same unresolved burden is now carried in a new differentiated degree.

That change is already the distinction.

## What remains open

v15 does **not** prove:

- derivability of E1,
- plurality from distinction,
- apartness,
- recursive higher-resolution restatement,
- truncation/frontier,
- locality/coherence,
- differentiability in the later geometric sense.
