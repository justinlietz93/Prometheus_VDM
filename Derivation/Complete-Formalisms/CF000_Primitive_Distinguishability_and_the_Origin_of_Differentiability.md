**CF00 does not derive identity/difference from below.**
What CF00 does is:

* **assume** a primitive carrier (\mathcal M) with local differential structure, and a (C^1) normalized representative state-family on it, with local (U(1)) redundancy
* then show that **within that already-given setting**, the representative contains a trivial self-parallel phase direction and a nontrivial horizontal remainder, via the (P_\parallel/P_\perp) split
* and only **after that** derive quotient-physical geometry and dynamics from the horizontal variation

So, CF00 already talks about:

* self-identity,
* trivial difference,
* and quotienting out sameness.

But it does so **after** these stronger primitives are already on the table:

* a carrier arena,
* differentiability,
* a representative section,
* local variation,
* inner-product structure,
* and local phase redundancy

This is why CF000 must be different.

CF000 has to begin **before** all of that. It cannot start from:

* a (C^1) carrier,
* a representative state-family,
* a projector,
* a phase direction,
* or even a pre-existing admissible local variation class.

Because in CF00, these are already primitive branch data. The primitive theorem says exactly that: the primitive ontology is exhausted by the representative state-family, the carrier arena, the fiberwise inner-product structure, and local (U(1)) redundancy.

So the distinction is:

* **CF00:** given a differentiable representative ontology, derive which local variation is physically redundant and which is quotient-physical.
* **CF000:** derive the possibility of any such representative ontology in the first place.

Or even more sharply:

* **CF00 asks:** once local variation exists, what part of it is sameness and what part is physical difference?
* **CF000 asks:** how can there be any lawful “this” and “not-this” at all, before local variation, representatives, or differentiability already exist?

So this sentence is basically right:

**anything that exists in and beyond CF00 cannot exist as a starting point in CF000.**

That means CF000 cannot inherit:

* representative self-identity,
* trivial phase redundancy,
* horizontal/vertical projectors,
* or admissible local variation

as primitives, because those are already CF00-level achievements or assumptions.
